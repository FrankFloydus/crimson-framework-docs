Use [`MoverComponent`](/api/classes/MoverComponent) to choose how the player's body turns and tune ground movement, air steering, and jump takeoff. For an AI or scripted body, attach [`Mover`](/api/classes/Mover) and supply a [`MotionRule`](/api/classes/MotionRule). [`Locomotion`](/api/classes/Locomotion) supplies standard rules, including grounded movement with a jump that starts on the floor.

## Classes

| Class | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [`Locomotion`](/api/classes/Locomotion) | Factory for built-in motion rules. |
| [`MotionRule`](/api/classes/MotionRule) | Editable settings for neutral or grounded movement. |
| [`Mover`](/api/classes/Mover) | Fixed-step movement for a CharacterBody3D or plain Node3D. |
| [`MoverComponent`](/api/classes/MoverComponent) | Editable movement settings on a character scene. |
