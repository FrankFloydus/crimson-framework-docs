Use Steam Inventory to inspect item definitions and stacks and request supported item changes. The client purchase service reads offers and follows orders through your game's authenticated commerce backend. The backend controls prices, settlement, and fulfillment.

**Module** [Steamworks](/api/modules/steamworks)
