**Inherits** `RefCounted`

**Availability** [Trusted Steam publisher addon](/steam/publisher).

One detached Valve report identity and its known app and target context.

Returned by `SteamPublisherModeration.report()` or within [`SteamCheatReportPage`](/api/classes/SteamCheatReportPage). A report is evidence for review. The trusted game retains the match and reviewer context.
