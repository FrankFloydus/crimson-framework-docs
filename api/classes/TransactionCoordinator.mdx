## Methods

### Instance methods

#### [`begin`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/begin)

Creates a writable transaction with a detached copy of the authority's `context`.

#### [`capture_snapshot`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/capture_snapshot)

Captures the entire store registry at one revision.

#### [`commit`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/commit)

Submits a transaction owned by this coordinator.

#### [`get_revision`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/get_revision)

Returns the coordinator's global commit revision, or `-1` from another thread.

#### [`get_store_revision`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/get_store_revision)

Returns the store's current committed revision, or `-1` when the store is unknown or accessed from another thread.

#### [`notify_restore_published`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/notify_restore_published)

Emits the deferred `committed` notification for the matching published revision.

#### [`prepare_restore`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/prepare_restore)

Validates a detached store map with exactly the registered store IDs.

#### [`publish_restore`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/publish_restore)

Publishes the candidate to all registered stores and advances the coordinator revision.

#### [`read_store`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/read_store)

Returns a detached copy of the last committed state.

#### [`register_store`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/register_store)

Registers a unique store ID, detached initial dictionary, and `prepare(current, operations, context)` callback.

#### [`register_validator`](/api/classes/TransactionCoordinator/methods/register_validator)

Registers a unique validator over existing `watched\_stores`.

## Signals

### `committed`

`committed(revision: int, stores: PackedStringArray)`

Emits after every prepared state is installed. The `revision` is the new coordinator revision and `stores` lists the changed store IDs. Observers can read committed data while nested mutation stays gated.
