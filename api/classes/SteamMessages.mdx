Steam-authenticated, bounded, app-scoped side-channel routing.

## Methods

### `authorize`

`authorize(steam_id: String, channels: StringName[]) -> SteamMessagePeerResult`

Authorizes one distinct remote individual SteamID64 in a standalone scope. Supply one to 16 distinct names of up to 32 ASCII characters. Names begin with a letter or digit and may also contain period, hyphen, or underscore. This grants local routing permission only. Game scopes require `for_peer()`.

### `for_peer`

`for_peer(peer_id: int) -> SteamMessagePeerResult`

Authorizes the Game's current Steam-verified participant using its configured channels. A guest resolves its authority. An authority resolves its admitted world-ready peers. The binding expires when the participant or current epoch retires.
