**Inherits** `RefCounted`

Exact scoped permission to exchange Steam Messages.

## Methods

### `close`

`close() -> Error`

Retires this exact local authorization and closes its framework-owned native channel. Native channels owned by other SDK consumers are preserved.

### `send`

`send(channel: StringName, bytes: PackedByteArray, delivery: SteamDelivery.Mode = 0) -> Error`

Queues one packet of 1 to 4096 bytes on an allowed app channel. Defaults to reliable ordered delivery. `OK` confirms local queue acceptance. Pending binding proof can queue up to eight reliable frames and 16 KiB. A remote binding change drops queued packets from the previous binding. Unreliable sends return `ERR_BUSY` until the exact binding is proven. An unconfigured channel returns `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.

## Signals

### `closed`

`closed(reason: Error)`

Emitted once when local authorization is withdrawn, its Game generation retires, its pending proof expires, or an exact send reports a connection failure.

### `received`

`received(channel: StringName, bytes: PackedByteArray, delivery: SteamDelivery.Mode)`

Emitted after copying and releasing the exact SDK packet. The app channel, identity, current scope, sequence, and size have been checked.
