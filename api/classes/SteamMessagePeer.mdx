## Signals

### `closed`

`closed(reason: Error)`

Emitted once when local authorization is withdrawn, its Game generation retires, its pending proof expires, or an exact send reports a connection failure.

### `received`

`received(channel: StringName, bytes: PackedByteArray, delivery: SteamDelivery.Mode)`

Emitted after copying and releasing the exact SDK packet. The app channel, identity, current scope, sequence, and size have been checked.
