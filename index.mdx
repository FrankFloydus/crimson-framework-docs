Crimson Framework is a C++ GDExtension for Godot 4.7. Use `GameRuntime` to start a local session, host or join a shared world, or coordinate a match with scene travel and checkpoints. Your project supplies the scenes, player controls, gameplay rules, and saved state.

You can also use the modules independently. For example, use `SpatialInventory` for a grid inventory or `SaveCoordinator` to capture and restore a checkpoint. Steamworks services can run on their own or provide discovery, invitations, and multiplayer for a Crimson game.
